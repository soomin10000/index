jeff has one RTL-SDR dongle (RTL-SDR Blog V3) — it can feed only one of plane tracking, the ISM-band sniffer, ACARS, or the waterfall at a time. Switching stops the other three, resets the dongle over USB, and verifies the new mode is actually receiving before calling it done — that check takes up to ~45s for plane tracking (waiting for a real decoded aircraft) and a few seconds for the others (checking the tuner opened cleanly), so a switch isn't instant.

Reading the waterfall: it shows the past, not a replay button — it scrolls continuously, so a streak partway down the screen already happened and won't play back. Click a streak still forming at the top edge to catch something live, or click any frequency and wait for the next transmission there. If you hear nothing, check the volume slider isn't too low and the squelch (SQ) isn't set so high it's muting weak signals.
