Passive ISM-band inventory off jeff's RTL-SDR dongle (433.92 / 868.3 MHz, hopped every 60s). Picks up nearby weather stations, TPMS, doorbells and similar consumer gear — including neighbours' devices, not just yours. These sensors broadcast one-way and unencrypted by design (they don't know or care who's listening), so seeing them here isn't snooping — it's how the protocol has always worked.
